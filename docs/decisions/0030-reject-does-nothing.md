# 0030: REJECT only logs

**Who decided:** Ionel. 2026-11-05.

**Decision.**
On REJECT, AECA logs it and does nothing else. The original email keeps its label.

**Why.** The spec gives no further reason.

**Rejected.**
- Archiving, relabelling or replying to the requester on REJECT (inferred).

**Cost.** The spec states none.

**Source:** [the spec](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-05-aeca-design.md), §4.5.
